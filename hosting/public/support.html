Support

Need help with the Derby Bins app? Below are the questions we are asked most often. If yours is not here, email contactxomo@gmail.com.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Common questions

My bin wasn't collected or is missing.

Use Report an issue in the app, and we will check it against your council's published information. We are not able to report a missed bin to your council on your behalf. If the collection is urgent, contact your council directly — the link to its waste service is on our Official Information Sources page.

The schedule or bin colour looks wrong.

Collection dates and bin colours come from your council's own published information. If something looks wrong, send a report from the app, or email us with your postcode, and we will check it against the council's information. If it is our bug rather than theirs, we will fix the app.

How do I change my address or postcode?

Open Settings in the app and change your address. Your new collections load automatically. Your new address replaces the old one on your device — we do not keep a history of previous addresses.

My reminders aren't firing.

Check that notifications are switched on for Derby Bins. On Android you also need to allow Alarms & reminders, and turning off battery optimisation for the app stops scheduled reminders being delayed or dropped while the phone is asleep.

Which councils are supported?

All nine: Derby City Council, Erewash Borough Council, Amber Valley Borough Council, High Peak Borough Council, Derbyshire Dales District Council, Bolsover District Council, Chesterfield Borough Council, South Derbyshire District Council and North East Derbyshire District Council. The sources page links to the official page behind each one.

Why does Bolsover or North East Derbyshire ask me to pick a calendar?

Those two councils do not run an address-based lookup. Bolsover District Council publishes two annual collection calendars, Calendar A and Calendar B, each covering a named list of towns. North East Derbyshire District Council does the same with a North area calendar and a South area calendar. There is no lookup for your address to hit, so the app asks which of those areas your town is in. Pick the one your town is listed under on the council's own page, which is linked from the sources page.

Is the app official?

No. Derby Bins is an independent utility app. It is not affiliated with, authorised by, sponsored by, or endorsed by any local council or government body.
