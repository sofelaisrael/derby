Official Information Sources

Derby Bins is an independent utility app. It is not affiliated with, authorised by, sponsored by, or endorsed by any local council or government body.

Collection information shown in Derby Bins is obtained from official local-authority websites and services. Use the links below to verify information for your area.

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Which councils this covers, and why

Derby Bins covers nine councils: Amber Valley, Bolsover, Chesterfield, Derbyshire Dales, Erewash, High Peak, North East Derbyshire and South Derbyshire, plus Derby City Council.

Derby City Council is a unitary authority. It is not part of the two-tier system that covers the rest of the county, which is why a list of "Derbyshire councils" normally reads as eight and this one reads as nine.

Derbyshire County Council does not publish bin collection days. Collecting household bins is the job of the district and borough councils listed above, and Derbyshire County Council is not a source for anything in this app.

This list reflects the councils as they stood on 8 October 2026. Local government reorganisation across Derbyshire has been proposed and was paused in September 2026, so the list may change; this page will be updated when it does.

You can check which councils are responsible for your area on GOV.UK.

Official council source

Derby City Council

What this source provides

Derby City Council's postcode page, which hands off to a bin-day search: you enter your postcode, pick your address and see your next collection.

Where Derby Bins gets the data

Derby Bins reads collection dates from that bin-day search, which the council serves from secure.derby.gov.uk.

https://www.derby.gov.uk/environment-and-planning/recycling-and-waste/your-bin-collections/all-about-your-bin-day/when-is-my-bin-day (opens in a new tab)

Erewash Borough Council

What this source provides

Erewash Borough Council's postcode widget, which returns your collection days, and a separate year-round calendar page listing them.

Where Derby Bins gets the data

Derby Bins reads collection dates from the council's postcode widget and from its collection-dates fragment.

Amber Valley Borough Council

What this source provides

Amber Valley Borough Council's address lookup, which returns your refuse and recycling round, how often it is collected, your next collections, and full-year refuse, recycling, garden and food waste calendars.

Where Derby Bins gets the data

Derby Bins reads collection dates through the council's own SOAP property-lookup and collection web services, hosted at info.ambervalley.gov.uk.

https://www.ambervalley.gov.uk/rubbish-waste-and-recycling/bin-collection-dates (opens in a new tab)

High Peak Borough Council

High Peak Borough Council's calendar-style dashboard, showing the next collection for each of your bin types.

Derbyshire Dales District Council

What this source provides

Derbyshire Dales District Council's "Apply: when is my collection" self-service form: you enter a postcode, select your address and submit. The council publishes no equivalent page of its own, so this form is the entry point.

Derby Bins reads collection dates from that same self-serve form on selfserve.derbyshiredales.gov.uk.

Official council source

Bolsover District Council

What this source provides

Bolsover District Council publishes two annual collection calendars, Calendar A and Calendar B, each covering a named list of towns, plus a self-service "Check your bin day" form. It is area-based, not address-based.

Where Derby Bins gets the data

Derby Bins reads collection dates from the council's annual calendars. For Bolsover it asks which area covers your town; it does not search for your address.

https://selfservice.bolsover.gov.uk/service/Check_your_Bin_Day (opens in a new tab)

Chesterfield Borough Council

What this source provides

Chesterfield Borough Council's finder, where you enter a postcode, choose an address and see your next collection. Reasons for a missed bin appear only once you are signed in to My Chesterfield.

Where Derby Bins gets the data

Derby Bins reads collection dates from the council's public postcode and address lookup, reached through the council's anonymous Aura endpoints at myaccount.chesterfield.gov.uk rather than through the page linked above.

https://www.chesterfield.gov.uk/bins-and-recycling/bin-collections/check-bin-collections.aspx (opens in a new tab)

South Derbyshire District Council

Official council source

North East Derbyshire District Council

What this source provides

North East Derbyshire District Council's published North and South collection calendars, with the villages listed for each zone. It is area-based, not address-based.

Where Derby Bins gets the data

Derby Bins reads collection dates from those published calendars, and sends your postcode to Ordnance Survey Places only when it has to decide which of the two areas applies to you.

https://www.ne-derbyshire.gov.uk/bins-and-recycling/bin-collection-dates (opens in a new tab)

Other information sources

  • Weather information is provided by Open-Meteo (opens in a new tab), using a fixed coordinate for your council area rather than your location.
  • Open-Meteo data is provided under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence (opens in a new tab).
  • Ordnance Survey Places (opens in a new tab) is used by one council only — North East Derbyshire District Council — to decide which of its North and South collection calendars applies to your area. The other eight councils' address search runs entirely on the council's own website and never reaches Ordnance Survey. A postcode is sent to a public address gazetteer, not a location lookup.
  • Reports you submit in the app are written to a private Google spreadsheet controlled by Derby Bins. No council receives them.

If a collection looks wrong

If a collection looks wrong, use the council waste service linked above for your area to check it and to report a missed bin. Derby Bins is not a council service and cannot change a collection.

You can also tell us about a problem at contactxomo@gmail.com and we will check it against the council's information.
